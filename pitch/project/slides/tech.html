Amazon's managed AI services were switched off in our hackathon lab, so we self-host open models. In production: AWS Dublin region (eu-west-1).

Speaker: Mohd Wafa (3:35 to 3:55). "Under the hood, it's all on AWS. Amazon's own AI services were switched off in our hackathon account, so we self-hosted open models on an EC2 server: Whisper for speech, Meta's NLLB for translation, a second model that checks the meaning, and Piper for the voices. Audio never leaves the hospital's cloud and is never stored. Every sentence goes to a DynamoDB audit log and exports as a FHIR record. About three seconds per double-checked sentence. We tested 37 languages on 20 medical sentences each, kept the 28 that passed, gave each one the translation model that scored best, and removed 9, including Mandarin, which turned appendicitis into tachycardia."
