Demo (1:40 to 3:15). Mohd Wafa narrates. Chijindu Okafor holds the phone (clinician). Bakht holds the iPad (patient screen). Eriitunu Adesioye holds the laptop on Live audit, angled to the judges. Setup before judging: phone has a Standard Consultation open (Ukrainian, locked); iPad has joined as Patient screen, tapped Start and consented; laptop has joined as Live audit. 1. Chijindu Okafor (phone, mic): "Is the pain at you? Have you had anything to eat or drink since midnight?" Show the plain-English rewrite and the highlighted details, then tap Confirm and translate. The iPad speaks Ukrainian. 2. Bakht (iPad): tap Demo, then "Yes, my stomach hurts on the right side, I'm allergic to penicillin". The phone shows English and pins "Allergy: penicillin". 3. Bakht taps pain 7 on the faces scale, then taps the lower right of the tummy on the body map and the green tick. The phone shows "Pain 7/10" and "Pain: lower right abdomen", with the body highlighted. 4. Chijindu Okafor: "Take two 500 milligram paracetamol tablets every six hours. Do not take more than eight in 24 hours." Show the critical-detail checkbox, then the green ticks for numbers, "not" and the medicine. 5. Chijindu Okafor: "We need your consent for the operation." The screen turns red. Tap Request interpreter; the iPad tells the patient, in Ukrainian, that an interpreter is coming. Tap "Open the interpreter's brief": the interpreter starts with the allergy, the pain score, where it hurts and every flagged sentence. 6. Eriitunu Adesioye turns the laptop: every line, tier and check is already in the audit log. Tap FHIR to show the record export. If the Wi-Fi fails: open Offline phrasebook from the home screen. Checked phrases, tap answers, pain scale and body map all work without a connection, which is itself a feature to point out. Otherwise switch to the backup video and keep talking.
