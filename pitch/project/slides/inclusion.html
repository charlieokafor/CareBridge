Inclusion and ethics

Designed for every patient.

Their own script

Українська, العربية, Română. No flags: language is not nationality.

Spoken aloud

Every line is voiced, for patients who can't read.

Tap to answer

Yes or no, a pain scale with faces, and a body map: tap where it hurts.

A human, any time

One tap, in the patient's own words. No more children interpreting.

Consent first

The patient is told it's a computer, in their language, before it starts.

Private by design

Staff-only access. Audio never stored. No neighbour interpreting.

Speaker: Bakht Zia Khan (4:20 to 4:30). "It's built for every patient: their language in their own script, never a flag; every line spoken aloud for patients who can't read; tap answers, a pain scale with faces and a body map, so a patient can show where it hurts without a single word; a human interpreter one tap away; consent before anything starts; and audio is never stored."
