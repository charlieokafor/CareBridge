What Consultation Mode is (for you, not to read out) Emergency Mode is hands-free: it translates both ways on its own, instantly. Consultation Mode (on screen: Standard Consultation) is for planned visits like check-ups, clinics and ward rounds, when there is time to get every word right.
  1. Two screens, one conversation. The doctor uses a phone; the patient has their own screen (the iPad) in their language.
  2. The doctor speaks, then reviews. CareBridge shows what it heard, with doses, medicines and “not” highlighted. The doctor can edit it, and must tick that the highlighted details are right.
  3. Then it goes to the patient. It is translated, numbers and medicines are checked, and it is shown and read aloud on the patient’s screen.
  4. The patient answers by speaking, or by tapping answers, a pain scale or a body map. Everything is saved to the record.
